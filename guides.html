Decision guide

Free vs. paid: upgrade for leverage, not novelty.

A free plan is enough when it solves the job without creating a new bottleneck. Pay when the upgrade removes repeated friction, not simply because a product has more features.

01 · Stay free

The job already fits

Keep the free plan when the core workflow is predictable and the limits do not interrupt delivery.

02 · Upgrade

The bottleneck is repeated

Pay when a higher limit, faster export, or better handoff saves meaningful time every week.

03 · Reconsider

The tool adds work

A richer plan is not valuable if setup, maintenance, or lock-in becomes the new problem.
