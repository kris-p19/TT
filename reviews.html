AI thinking

ChatGPT

↗

A flexible starting point when the work is still taking shape. It is useful for turning rough notes into a brief, exploring angles, and getting a usable first draft.

Why it earns a place

  • Low friction for everyday thinking
  • Good for moving from idea to outline
  • Useful when the problem is still vague

Keep in mind

  • It needs a clear brief to shine
  • Important output deserves a second pass
  • Do not outsource the final judgment

↗

A strong fit for thoughtful collaboration around longer documents, project context, and work where the quality of the conversation matters as much as the answer.

Why it earns a place

  • Comfortable with long context
  • Good for shaping a working draft

Notion

↗

A flexible place to keep the context behind the work: notes, project plans, decisions, and the small details that otherwise disappear into chat history.

Why it earns a place

  • One home for mixed content
  • Easy to shape around your process
  • Useful for solo and small teams

Keep in mind

  • Too much setup can slow you down
  • Decide where the source of truth lives

Automation

Zapier

↗

A straightforward way to connect the tools already in your workflow and remove the small handoffs that happen every week.

Why it earns a place

  • Quick wins for simple triggers
  • Large integration ecosystem
  • Good first automation layer

Keep in mind

  • Complex flows get complex
  • Watch task limits and plan changes
  • Automate only what is predictable

Design

↗

A fast route from a rough idea to a visual that is ready to share, especially when you need to keep the brand looking consistent across a busy week.

Why it earns a place

  • Fast path to polished output
  • Good templates for solo marketing
  • Easy to share and review

  • Advanced systems need discipline
