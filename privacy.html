Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Information you choose to share

If you contact Stackwise, the information you provide may include your name, email address, and message. We use that information only to respond, answer a correction, or discuss a partnership.

2. Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense advertising technology. The ad script is not loaded until you choose Allow ads in the privacy banner. If you allow it, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and understand how visitors use the site. Depending on your region and settings, advertising may be personalized.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your Google Ads Settings or the relevant third-party provider. Browsers can also reject or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the site works. Use the Privacy choices button to change your saved advertising choice.

3. Analytics

If analytics are added, the site may collect aggregate information about visits, pages viewed, approximate location, browser type, and referral source. We will keep analytics limited to understanding what content is useful.

4. External links

Pages may link to independent websites and services. Once you follow an external link, that provider’s own privacy practices apply. Stackwise is not responsible for the content or data practices of third-party sites.

5. Commercial relationships

If Stackwise links to a paid service and earns a commission, that does not change the price you pay. Any commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly when it affects a recommendation.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to share personal information, clear cookies through your browser, and opt out of personalized advertising using the controls provided by the relevant provider. The Privacy choices button stores your advertising selection in your browser so you can revisit it later.

7. Updates

This policy may change as the site, its services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of the page will show when the latest version was published.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the site, contact stackwise@fintechxhub.com. Please include the page URL and the question so the editorial desk can respond clearly.

Good to know

This policy describes the site’s current data flows. If analytics, a newsletter, or another advertising provider is added, update this page and the consent choices before enabling it.
