Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information you choose to share

If you contact Stackwise or subscribe to an update, the information you provide may include your name, email address, and message. We use that information only to respond, provide the requested update, or improve the site.

2. Advertising and cookies

This site loads Google AdSense advertising technology. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and understand how visitors use the site. Depending on the visitor’s region and privacy choices, advertising may be personalized.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your Google Ads Settings or the relevant third-party provider. Browsers can also reject or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the site works. A consent management platform may be required for visitors in some regions before personalized advertising is enabled.

3. Analytics

If analytics are added, the site may collect aggregate information about visits, pages viewed, approximate location, browser type, and referral source. We will keep analytics limited to understanding what content is useful.

4. External links

Pages may link to independent websites and services. Once you follow an external link, that provider’s own privacy practices apply. Stackwise is not responsible for the content or data practices of third-party sites.

5. Commercial relationships

If Stackwise links to a paid service and earns a commission, that does not change the price you pay. Any commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly when it affects a recommendation.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to share personal information, clear cookies through your browser, and opt out of personalized advertising using the controls provided by the relevant provider.

7. Updates

This policy may change as the site, its services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of the page will show when the latest version was published.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the site, contact stackwise@fintechxhub.com. Please include the page URL and the question so the editorial desk can respond clearly.

Good to know

This page is designed to be a clear starting point. Before publishing with advertising or analytics enabled, the publisher should review it against their actual data practices and local requirements.
